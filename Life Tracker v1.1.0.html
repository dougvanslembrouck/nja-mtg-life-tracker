v1.1.0 EDH Pod Life Tracker · October 20, 2026

Commanders, Crowns & Tax

Three new features to make the tracker feel like your table: your commander's art behind your life total, a crown for the monarch, and a counter for commander tax.

Commander art backgrounds

Tap Choose commander under any player's name and search about 3,500 commander-legal cards. Pick one, and that seat's background becomes the card's art, with the artist credited.

  • Search by any part of the name: tchalla, black pan and sauron lord all work
  • Color identity pips next to every result
  • Art comes from the newest standard printing, skipping promos and showcase, extended-art or etched versions
  • Each seat remembers its commander on your device, and Log Game records who played what
  • Search works offline. Without internet, the seat keeps its land background
  • Use land background switches back anytime

Monarch

A crown button sits next to I'm Dead on every player card, so the whole table can see who draws the extra card at end of turn.

  • Tap to crown a player. Their card gets a gold border and a gold crown
  • Only one monarch at a time, so crowning someone takes it from whoever had it
  • An eliminated monarch loses the crown. Tap it onto the new monarch
  • Match history marks the game's final monarch with ♛
In the app
♛ Arcane is the monarch

Commander tax

Each player now has a Tax counter beside their commander. Tap + every time the commander is cast again from the command zone.

  • Counts in steps of 2, so the number is exactly the extra mana the next cast costs
  • Turns amber once a player has tax, and can't go below 0
  • Resets with the game and is saved in match history
In the app TAX−4+ Third cast costs 4 more 0→2→4→6

Also in this release

  • Match history now shows each player's commander, the monarch and commander tax
  • Player names in match history are now displayed safely, so special characters can't break the page
  • On phones in 4-player mode, the commander button and tax counter stack so neither gets cut off
  • New tools/update-commanders.html rebuilds the commander list from Scryfall when new sets release

Upgrading

Replace index.html and add these new files next to it:

commanders.jsThe bundled commander list
tools/update-commanders.htmlRebuilds the list when new sets come out

Your existing match history carries over. Older games just won't show a commander, monarch or tax.

Data format

Each player in exported match history now includes three new fields, highlighted below.

{
  "id": 1,
  "name": "Arcane",
  "lifeLeft": 12,
  "isDead": false,
  "isMonarch": true,
  "commanderTax": 4,
  "commander": "T'Challa, the Black Panther"
}
